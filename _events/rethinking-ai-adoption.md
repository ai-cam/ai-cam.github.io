  **Monday 25 January 2027; 10:00–16:30; Downing College, Cambridge**


  AI adoption has become a central objective of economic and innovation policy. But how well do established approaches to technology diffusion apply to AI?


  AI presents some distinctive challenges. Its capabilities are evolving rapidly, adoption can happen informally and without organisational oversight, and improvements in individual tasks do not necessarily translate into wider productivity gains or economic benefits. These characteristics raise questions about how we understand, measure, and support effective adoption.


  This ai@cam Policy Lab will bring together researchers, policymakers, and people with practical experience of developing and adopting AI to explore a central question:


  **What, if anything, makes AI adoption a different policy problem from previous waves of technology adoption? And what follows for the policies and institutions intended to support it?**


  Through a series of participatory discussions, we'll examine the evidence on AI adoption, explore the organisational and institutional factors that shape its outcomes, and consider the implications for policy.


  This is a small, invitation-led workshop, designed to support substantive discussion across research, policy, and practice.
