  **How should we approach the opportunities and challenges of AI in teaching and learning at Cambridge?**


  Join ai@cam for the first AI for Teaching and Learning Community Meetup, bringing together colleagues from across the University to share experiences, explore ideas and begin a conversation about how we want to use AI in education.


  The event will provide an opportunity to hear about projects and initiatives taking shape across Cambridge, meet others working on related questions, and explore the opportunities, challenges and priorities that should inform our collective approach.


  Whether you're already experimenting with AI in teaching and learning, undertaking research in this area, or interested in what these technologies might mean for education, we'd welcome your participation.


  This is the first in what we hope will become an ongoing conversation and community of practice across the University. It is open to members of staff at the University.
